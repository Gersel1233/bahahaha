Spiis

Live in production Restaurant · ordering & table booking · spiis.dk
The problem before us

The kitchen had no overview — and it cost them every single day.

  • Guests called and interrupted the chef mid-service.
  • Emails with orders and party requests went unanswered.
  • Nobody knew what to cook — or how much of it.
What we built

A complete digital platform for Spiis, built from scratch and running straight on spiis.dk:

Commission-free online ordering

Guests pre-order the daily dish or the full menu up to two weeks ahead. Stock counts down and sold-out items close themselves — overselling is impossible.

Event & party booking

Requests land straight in the kitchen’s pipeline, and a confirmed event blocks the day automatically.

A live kitchen dashboard

Run from the staff’s own phones — every order arrives instantly, with a day-by-day production plan and one shared overview.

Full self-service control

Menus, prices, opening hours, order windows and holiday mode — edited in seconds, live immediately.

News & specials

The kitchen posts its own announcements and pre-orderable specials — think Christmas platters — straight to the front page.

Swipe →

One system, one fixed price, zero commission — every krone of every order stays with the restaurant.
