01 Where you create
02 The standard you're signing

Fyon shows people an honest, achievable version of themselves. Your content carries the same honesty — these rules protect your audience, your payouts and the brand.

Disclosure & traffic

  • ✓Tag every promotional post #ad or #partner, clearly and up front — it's the law, and it builds trust.
  • ✓Use only your own link and code.
  • ✕No self-purchases, bought traffic, spam or coupon-site posting — commissions from them are void.

Honesty about results

  • ✓Present Fyon renders as what they are — an AI visualization of your achievable direction ("my Fyon potential"), never as a real photo of an outcome.
  • ✓Edit freely for pacing, captions and format — the result itself stays exactly as Fyon showed it.
  • ✕No income or results guarantees, no fabricated before/afters, no exaggerating a render beyond what Fyon generated.

Audience & safety

  • ✓18+ audiences only — Fyon refuses minors by design, and your content does too.
  • ✓Sell becoming — aspiration, effort, honesty. That's the angle that converts anyway.
  • ✕No medical or surgical claims. No shame angles ("you're ugly without this"). No minors on screen — ever.

Violations end the partnership and void unpaid commissions. The full guidelines live in your dashboard after approval.

What to post

The reveal does the selling — your job is the moment around it. Every winning video is the same 20 seconds:

1
Hook — 0–3s

One line, spoken and on screen. Tease the reveal — never explain the app.

2
Reveal + reaction — 3–20s

Film yourself seeing your future self for the first time — that half-second of silence is the whole ad.

3
CTA — last 3s

“Link in bio — code YOURCODE · 10% off.” Say it, caption it, pin it.

Hooks that work

“I uploaded 2 photos and this is me in 4 months…”
“It refused to change my eyes. That's when I knew it was serious.”
“I wasn't ready to meet my future self.”
“Day 14 of following the plan my future self gave me.”

Everything else is in your Library — inspiration videos, screen recordings, overlay material, edit inspo and copy-paste hooks, captions & scripts.
